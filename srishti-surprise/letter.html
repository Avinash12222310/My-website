The Letter

Dear Srishti,

I wanted to make you something instead of just saying it out loud, because you deserve to have it written down somewhere you can come back to.

You've got this way of showing up for people without making a big deal out of it. I've watched you do it for other people plenty of times, and I don't think you give yourself enough credit for it.

I know we don't always say the soft stuff to each other directly — so consider this me saying it through a slightly overengineered website instead. You matter to me more than I probably show.

There's more waiting for you — keep going.

— Avinash
