The Letter

Dear Srishti,

I wanted to make you something you could come back to, instead of letting your birthday pass with one quick message.

Being your sibling means I've had a front-row seat to the person you are: thoughtful, determined, and completely your own. I hope you give yourself credit for how far you've come.

We may not say the heartfelt stuff every day, but I am proud of you, grateful you're my sister, and always in your corner.

Here's to a brilliant 26th year. There's more waiting for you — keep going.

— Avinash
