Twenty-six looks good on you

No big life checklist. Just a few good things I hope find their way to you this year.

From your sibling, with a lot of pride: twenty-six little wishes for the year ahead. Keep the ones that fit; make the rest your own.

  1. 01More mornings that start slowly.
  2. 02Work that notices what you bring to it.
  3. 03People who make room for the real you.
  4. 04At least one laugh-you-can't-stop day each week.
  5. 05Plans that excite you, and the courage to change them.
  6. 06Rest that doesn't need to be earned.
  7. 07Good food shared with your favourite people.
  8. 08Small wins you remember to celebrate.
  9. 09More confidence in the choices you already know are yours.
  10. 10New places, near or far, that make you curious.
  11. 11Kindness for yourself on the messy days.
  12. 12Space to try something just because it sounds fun.
  13. 13Friends who show up as happily as you do for them.
  14. 14Surprises that are actually the good kind.
  15. 15More moments where you feel proud of yourself.
  16. 16Boundaries that protect your peace.
  17. 17Music for every version of your mood.
  18. 18Enough quiet to hear what you really want.
  19. 19Projects that make time fly in the best way.
  20. 20People who cheer for you without keeping score.
  21. 21More ordinary days that turn out pretty great.
  22. 22Permission to be a beginner at something new.
  23. 23Comfort in knowing you don't have to have it all figured out.
  24. 24Stories that become family favourites.
  25. 25A year that feels like it belongs to you.
  26. 26And always, a sibling in your corner. Happy birthday, Srishti.
